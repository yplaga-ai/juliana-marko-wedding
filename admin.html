People & Access

Start here. Add everyone helping on the day — bride, groom, maid of honour, best man, parents, coordinator, photographer, family. Whoever you add here becomes a pick-able name in the Who dropdowns on the Run Sheet and Task Board below. Add a private note and print that person’s pack (their schedule + tasks + notes). To give someone their own live view, set a password (type your own or hit Suggest, and change it anytime), tick what they’re allowed to see, then Copy link and send them the link — tell them their password separately. They open the link and log in with their name + password. Saves automatically.

Wedding Day — Run Sheet

makeup, hair, photographer & more

Your timeline for the day. Add the time, pick who it's for from your People list (or “Everyone”), an optional location, and what's happening. Add people in People & Access above and they appear in this dropdown. Print prints the group sheet, or pick a person to print just theirs; Everyone's own sheets prints a booklet with one page per person. Everything saves automatically.

Transport

Cars, shuttles and lifts on the day. Each leg is one trip — getting to the venue and getting from it are two separate legs, so add one for each direction. Give it a time and a From → To, and it automatically drops into your Run Sheet (and each person’s pack / personal login) at that time. Tick Guests to also show a leg on your wedding site (e.g. a shuttle home). Saves automatically.

Accommodation

Where you and the party are staying — and the places you’d recommend to guests. Add the check-in / check-out, who’s staying, the address and any confirmation number. Tick Guests to show a place on your wedding site (with its booking link) as a suggestion. Saves automatically.

Task Board

Who’s doing what, and where it’s up to — your working list. Assign a task to a person, give it a due date, and move it To do → In progress → Done. Each person only sees their own tasks on their personal link. Tick Day-of and the task also appears on the Day-of Checklist below (ticking it done in either place keeps both in sync). Saves automatically.

Day-of Checklist

Built automatically from the tasks you tick Day-of in the Task Board above (with who’s on it and when it’s due), plus supplier payments. Tick one off here and it’s marked Done on the board too. Edit the task text/assignee up in the Task Board. Everything saves automatically.

Gift Registry
